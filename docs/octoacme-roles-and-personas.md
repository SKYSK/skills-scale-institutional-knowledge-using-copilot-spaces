# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Project Sponsors

### Role Summary
Project Sponsors provide organizational backing and make high-level business decisions needed to move a project forward.

### Responsibilities
- Champion the project and help secure organizational support and resources
- Confirm alignment with business goals and resolve escalated business trade-offs
- Approve major scope, funding, or priority decisions within their authority
- Help remove organizational blockers raised by the Project Manager

### Goals
- Ensure the project remains aligned with its business purpose
- Enable timely decisions and sustained organizational support

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and sponsor updates
- Escalations and decisions coordinated with the Project Manager

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Project Manager on escalations, resources, and delivery constraints
- Align with the Product Manager on desired outcomes and priority trade-offs
- Provide direction and decisions to Developers, QA/Testing, and other stakeholders through the agreed project leads

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads or Architects guide technical direction and help the delivery team build reliable solutions that fit the project's architecture and integration needs.

### Responsibilities
- Guide architecture and technical design decisions
- Identify technical, scalability, and integration risks and recommend mitigations
- Support Developers with implementation guidance and technical reviews
- Contribute technical estimates and identify dependencies
- Promote maintainable designs and testability

### Goals
- Deliver solutions that are reliable, maintainable, and compatible with existing systems
- Surface technical risks and dependencies early

### Typical Communication
- Technical design documents and design reviews
- Engineering planning, estimation, and dependency discussions

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Collaborate with Developers on design, implementation, and code reviews
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on testability and technical validation
- Share estimates, dependencies, and risks with the Project Manager
- Advise the Product Manager on feasibility and technical trade-offs

---

## UX / Research Leads

### Role Summary
UX / Research Leads bring user needs and usability evidence into product discovery, design, and validation.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and conduct user research or usability evaluation
- Translate findings into user needs, design recommendations, and testable acceptance criteria
- Identify accessibility and usability considerations
- Validate proposed experiences with users and share evidence with the team

### Goals
- Ensure solutions address user needs and are understandable and usable
- Reduce the risk of building features that do not solve the intended problem

### Typical Communication
- Research findings and design reviews
- Discovery sessions and acceptance-criteria discussions

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager on problem framing, discovery, and prioritization
- Work with Developers to refine designs against technical constraints
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on usability and accessibility validation
- Share research insights with the Project Manager and relevant stakeholders

---

## Security / Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security / Privacy Leads identify security, privacy, and compliance needs and help teams address them throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Clarify applicable security, privacy, and compliance requirements
- Assess risks, including data handling and access concerns, and recommend mitigations
- Advise on appropriate security and privacy reviews or validation
- Escalate material risks through the established incident or risk process

### Goals
- Address security and privacy risks early and consistently
- Help ensure the delivered solution meets applicable requirements

### Typical Communication
- Risk reviews and security or privacy assessments
- Guidance and findings shared with delivery and product leads

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Developers to incorporate appropriate safeguards into designs and implementation
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on verification of security and privacy requirements
- Help the Project Manager track risks, mitigations, and escalation needs
- Advise the Product Manager on requirements and trade-offs that affect the product

---

## Release / Operations Leads

### Role Summary
Release / Operations Leads coordinate operational readiness so changes can be deployed, monitored, supported, and recovered safely.

### Responsibilities
- Define operational readiness needs, including monitoring and support handoffs
- Coordinate deployment plans, release checks, and rollback or mitigation plans
- Confirm that operational documentation and ownership are clear
- Help triage production issues and feed lessons back into delivery planning

### Goals
- Make releases predictable and observable
- Reduce service disruption and improve recovery when issues occur

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and deployment coordination
- Operational runbooks, monitoring updates, and incident communications

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Coordinate release timing, dependencies, and stakeholder updates with the Project Manager
- Work with Developers on deployment, monitoring, and rollback plans
- Partner with QA/Testing to confirm smoke tests and release checks are ready
- Coordinate with Security / Privacy Leads when operational controls or risks are relevant

---

## Assigning Roles

These roles describe responsibilities, not required team headcount. Depending on project size and context, one person may hold multiple roles or a responsibility may be assigned to an existing team member. The Project Manager and Product Manager should make ownership explicit during planning, involve relevant specialists early, and ensure each decision and delivery responsibility has a clear point of contact.
